# AI Marketing Team

Hire a five-person marketing team in 45 minutes. Built for founders who do not write code and do not want to learn to.

Give it one product or service. It gives you back a brand Design System, a positioning document, a map of where the open lane is in your space, a Hooks Database written for your product, and a finished social campaign with a UGC-style Sizzle Reel.

Then you keep the team.

---


---

## The team

| # | The hire | Minutes | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Designer** | 10 | Your Design System in Claude Design. Colors, type, and social templates already built in your brand. |
| 2 | **The Strategist** | 8 | Your positioning. The exact buyer, the problem in her words, and the one line that makes someone pick you. |
| 3 | **The Scout** | 7 | Your White Space Map. What's crowded, what's contested, and the lane nobody's standing in. |
| 4 | **The Copywriter** | 8 | Your Hooks Database. Two dozen opening lines written for your product, scored and sorted. You pick one. |
| 5 | **The Producer** | 8 | Your campaign. On-brand posts, captions, and a Sizzle Reel from the hook you chose. |

Each one writes a file to a folder called `marketing-team/`. That folder is yours. It's what makes this repeatable instead of a one-time chat.

---

## What you need

- A paid Claude account
- One product or service you want to sell
- Forty-five minutes
- For the Sizzle Reel only: a Magnific account. Optional. Without it you still get the complete shot list, which you can film on your phone.

---

## Doing it again

Open Claude. Say **build my marketing team**. Point it at something else. It already knows your brand, so the second run takes about twenty minutes.

---

## Under the hood

- `data/viral-patterns.md` — pre-analyzed outlier research across nine kinds of business. Refresh every 8 to 10 weeks; instructions are at the bottom of that file.
- `data/hook-craft.md` — the Four Corners framework.
- `data/voice.md` — how the system talks.
- `data/video-provider.md` — change one line to swap video engines. Magnific, Higgsfield, Runway, ElevenLabs, or none.
- `data/demo-brand.md` — the sample brand for anyone who arrives without a product.
- `facilitator/` — stage script and runbook for running this as a live workshop.
